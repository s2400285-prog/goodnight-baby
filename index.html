Thank you for being you, Julie. Thank you for your kindness, your thoughtfulness, your sweetness, and all the little things you do that make me feel cared for. Bisan simple lang nga things, baby, I appreciate them more than you know. Indi ko gusto nga ma-feel mo nga kinahanglan mo permi maghimo sang something big para mapasaya ako. And baby, if kapoy ka subong, please allow yourself to rest. You don't need to be strong every single moment. You deserve peaceful nights, happy mornings, and days when your heart feels light. Pahuway ka gid, ha? Indi magpuyat permi, kay I want you to take care of yourself too.

I wish I could give you a warm hug right now, kiss your forehead, fix your blanket, and tell you, "I'm here, baby. You can rest now." For now, bisan indi ako physically ara sa tupad mo, I hope this little page makes you feel loved. If I could send you one thing tonight, it would be a hug that lasts until you fall asleep. 🫂

Most importantly, I pray that God watches over you tonight. May He calm every worry in your mind, protect you while you sleep, and give you strength for tomorrow. May He bless your family and fill your home with peace. I pray that you wake up feeling refreshed, hopeful, and reminded of how loved you are.

Salamat gid sa tanan, baby Julie. Thank you for being part of my life. I may not always find the perfect words, but I will always try to remind you how much you mean to me. I hope we continue growing with God, learning to understand each other, and choosing kindness even when life gets difficult.

I love you so much, baby ko. More than these little words can explain. 💜

So tonight, close your eyes and let the worries of today rest. Indi na pag-overthink ang tanan, ha? Tomorrow is another day, and you don't have to figure everything out tonight. Dream sweetly, my pretty girl.

Goodnight, my favorite person. 🌙
Sweet dreams, baby Julie. ♡
